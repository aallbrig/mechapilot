# Threat model: Marketing Site measurement

Covers `scripts/site-budget`, `scripts/site-observe`, `scripts/site-no-overrides` and the html-validate, lychee and Lighthouse configs. The attacker is a page change that ships script or third-party loads while every site check stays green.

| ID | Attack class | Description | Found | Status | Mitigation | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SI-01 | Handler as the first attribute | The handler regex consumed the only space, so `<button onclick=...>` passed. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Handlers are matched in any position. | `test:test_site_budget_rejects_handler_as_first_attribute` |
| SI-02 | Parser differential: abrupt comment | `<!-->` closes a comment in browsers but not in the awk tokenizer. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Abrupt and bang-closed comments are rejected, and Chrome observes the page. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_abrupt_comment_script` |
| SI-03 | Parser differential: bang close | `--!>` closes a comment in browsers. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Rejected statically and observed in Chrome. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_bang_comment_close_img` |
| SI-04 | NUL truncation | BSD awk truncates a record at NUL, hiding what follows. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | NUL and other C0 controls are rejected. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_nul_before_script` |
| SI-05 | Foreign-content raw text | `title` and `textarea` inside SVG or MathML are normal elements, not raw text. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Content is not skipped inside foreign elements. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_svg_title_script` |
| SI-06 | MathML breakout | `<math><title><img>` loads a third party. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Observed in Chrome. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_math_title_img` |
| SI-07 | Control characters before a scheme | The URL parser strips leading C0 controls that the static check allowed. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Controls are stripped before the scheme test. | `test:test_site_budget_rejects_control_char_url` |
| SI-08 | Presentation-attribute URLs | An SVG `mask` attribute with `url(...)` loads a third party. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | `url(` in attribute values is rejected. | `test:test_site_budget_rejects_svg_mask_url` |
| SI-09 | SMIL animation | A `set` element swaps a third-party URL into `href` after load. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | `set` and `animate*` are rejected. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_svg_set_href` |
| SI-10 | Executable extensions | `.shtml` or `.xht` pages are served as HTML but were not scanned. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | An allow-list of build extensions. | `test:test_site_budget_rejects_shtml_file` |
| SI-11 | Encoding differential | A UTF-16 page hides `<script>` from a byte-oriented scan. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | UTF-8 without BOM or NUL is required. | `test:test_site_budget_rejects_utf16_page` |
| SI-12 | Allowlist keyed on a spoofable URL | A script's `sourceURL` comment made it look like the observer's own helper. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Exemptions do not depend on a script's URL. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_svg_xhtml_script_spoofing_pptr_url` |
| SI-13 | Action after the settle window | A delayed navigation happens after observation stops. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Caught as script execution, not as navigation. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_svg_script_delayed_navigation` |
| SI-14 | XML entity expansion | An internal DTD entity carries script past the static check. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | DOCTYPE internal subsets are rejected and `.xml` is loaded in Chrome. | `test:test_site_observe_rejects_xml_entity_script` |
| SI-15 | Observer fails open on crash | A crashed renderer still reported every page loaded. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Load timeouts and target crashes are failures. | `test:test_site_observe_fails_closed_when_renderer_crashes` |
| SI-16 | Observer hangs on browser death | A dead browser left promises pending forever. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | Socket close and process exit reject pending calls and exit 2. | `test:test_site_observe_fails_closed_when_browser_dies` |
| SI-17 | Auto-read ignore files | `.lycheeignore` and `.htmlvalidateignore` are read automatically and silence findings. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | mitigated | They are refused anywhere in the tree. | `test:test_site_no_overrides_rejects_lycheeignore` |
| SI-18 | Test-only fault switches | A test-only environment switch can turn a finding into a pass when set by config. | [#47](https://github.com/aallbrig/mechapilot/pull/47) | open | Planned: harden or remove test-only overrides. | #60 |
